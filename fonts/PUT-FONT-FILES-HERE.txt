Brand fonts for furryheartsofjo.org
Put the font files in this folder with exactly these names:
  PestaRakyat.otf            (titles)
  GTWalsheim-Light.woff2     (body text)
  GTWalsheim-Regular.woff2
  GTWalsheim-Medium.woff2    (subtitles, buttons)
GT Walsheim needs a WEB licence from Grilli Type to be used on a website.
Until the files are here, the site shows Bagel Fat One and Figtree instead.
